F01  ∀s (parking_space(s) → ∃z (parking_zone(z) ∧ located_in(s, z)))
     Every parking space lies in some parking zone.
     Horn: Not Horn: ∃ in the conclusion. Mirrors DL axiom T15.
     Model check: holds
F02  ∀s ∀z1 ∀z2 ((located_in(s, z1) ∧ located_in(s, z2)) → z1 = z2)
     A space lies in at most one zone (located_in is functional).
     Horn: Not Horn without equality; enforced by the frame facet cardinality=single.
     Model check: holds
F03  ∀x (person(x) → (student(x) ∨ employee(x) ∨ visitor(x)))
     Every person is a student, an employee or a visitor.
     Horn: Not Horn: three positive literals in the conclusion (covering axiom).
     Model check: holds
F04  ∀x ¬(student(x) ∧ employee(x))
     Nobody is both a student and an employee.
     Horn: Horn denial clause IC4; DL axiom D01.
     Model check: holds
F05  ∀x ∀y ∀s ((allocate(x, s) ∧ allocate(y, s)) → x = y)
     A space is allocated to at most one person.
     Horn: Horn denial clause IC1 (uses ≠ instead of =).
     Model check: holds
F06  ∀x ∀s (allocate(x, s) → ¬space_status(s, occupied))
     An occupied space is never allocated.
     Horn: Horn denial clause IC3.
     Model check: holds
F07  ∀x ∀s ((allocate(x, s) ∧ space_type(s, accessible)) → accessibility_badge(x, yes))
     Whoever is allocated an accessible bay holds a badge.
     Horn: Consequence of R16 and R28 (a safety property, checked on the model). Same meaning as DL axiom T19.
     Model check: holds
F08  ∀x (authorized_driver(x) → ∃p (has_permit(x, p) ∧ permit_valid(p)))
     Every authorized driver holds at least one valid permit.
     Horn: Follows from R06-R11; the ∃ cannot be written as a Horn conclusion.
     Model check: holds
F09  ∃s (located_in(s, zone_a) ∧ space_free(s))
     There is at least one free space in the student zone.
     Horn: An existential query; backward chaining answers it with ?- located_in(S, zone_a), space_free(S).
     Model check: holds
F10  ∀x ((visitor(x) ∧ ¬∃p has_permit(x, p)) → ¬authorized_driver(x))
     A visitor with no permit or pass is not an authorized driver.
     Horn: Not derivable in classical FOL from R01-R28 alone; true under the closed-world assumption the Horn engine uses (negation as failure).
     Model check: holds
F11  ∀x ∀s (allocate(x, s) → ∃t requests(x, s, t))
     Every allocation answers a request.
     Horn: Follows from R28, whose body starts with requests(X, S, T).
     Model check: holds
